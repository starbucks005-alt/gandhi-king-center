Peace Partners

Walking alongside.

Organizations and institutions whose work overlaps with ours, with whom we collaborate on programming, exhibits, and the Season for Nonviolence.

Current partners

Dayton International Peace Museum — Our local advocacy anchor. Founded in 2004 after the Dayton Peace Accords. Co-host of Peace Camp and the founding 2024 Season for Nonviolence kickoff.

The King Center, Atlanta — Founded by Coretta Scott King in 1968. Source of the Nonviolence365® training curriculum we partner on.

Bosserman Center for Conflict Resolution, Salisbury University — Co-host of the 2025 Season for Nonviolence kickoff. Home of board Ambassador Dr. Brian Polkinghorn.

The Lightning Strike, Chicago — Co-host of the 2026 Season for Nonviolence kickoff.

M.K. Gandhi Institute for Nonviolence — Founded by Arun Gandhi in 1991. The institutional cousin in the Gandhi family's American work.

Become a Peace Partner

The Gandhi-King Center welcomes partnership inquiries from organizations whose mission advances peace, nonviolence, civil rights, conflict resolution, interfaith collaboration, or related work. Partnership can take several forms, from co-hosting Season events to sharing exhibits to amplifying programming.
