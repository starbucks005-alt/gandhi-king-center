Teach the methods.

Nonviolence is a practice before it is a philosophy. We partner with the institutions that already do this work well, and we extend their reach into Dayton and beyond.

Ongoing

Peace 101

Dayton International Peace Museum · All ages

A foundational program designed to engage students of all ages in the principles of peace, kindness, and social justice. Hosted at the Dayton International Peace Museum.

Ongoing

Nonviolence365® Training

The King Center · Atlanta, Georgia

Founded in 1968 by Coretta Scott King, The King Center provides extensive peace education through its Nonviolence365® training. Our partnership extends access to the curriculum developed around Dr. King's philosophy of nonviolent conflict resolution.

Coming next

An education layer for schools and students is in development. The aim is curriculum-grade material that brings the Gandhi-King traditions into classrooms responsibly. If you teach, lead a school, or design educational programming, we want to hear from you.
