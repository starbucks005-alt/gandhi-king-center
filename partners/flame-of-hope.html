Partners · India

Flame of Hope.

A partner organization rooted in India and walking alongside the Gandhi-King Center across two continents. We met them on the Gandhi Legacy Tour. They came to Dayton. The partnership continues.

How we met

The Gandhi-King Center came to know Flame of Hope during the Gandhi Legacy Tour in India, the same tour where Dr. Oroszi traveled with Tushar Gandhi and Dr. Ellis as the Center’s founding U.S. representative. Flame of Hope was already doing their work in their own communities. The Center’s response was straightforward: stand alongside, name the partnership publicly, make the work louder.

Coming to Dayton

Flame of Hope traveled to Dayton, Ohio, to meet with the Center’s board in person and to walk through the city where the Center is headquartered. International partnerships often die in the gap between the trip out and the trip back. This one did not.
