# Voice capture kit — Joel and Greg

For recording today. Phone voice memo is perfectly fine. The goal is two to three minutes of clean,
natural speech per person, with consent recorded at the start.

## First, the consent line (record this before anything else)

Have each person say, in their own words or like this:

> "My name is [full name]. Today is [date]. I give the Gandhi-King Center and Dr. Terry Oroszi
> permission to use this recording of my voice to create my AI agent. I understand I can change
> my mind at any time."

## Recording tips (for a clean clone)

- Quiet room, door closed. Turn off fans, AC, TV, music.
- Hold the phone about a hand's width from the mouth. Voice memo app is fine.
- Aim for 2 to 3 minutes of clear speech. Natural pace, normal warmth, a little variation is good.
- If a phone rings or a dog barks, pause, then repeat that sentence.
- A mix is best: read the short passage once or twice, then just talk naturally for a minute.

## Shared read passage (good clean sample, on the mission)

> "Nonviolence is not the absence of conflict. It is a way of living, practiced in the smallest
> choices, every day. I believe every person carries the power to be a peacemaker, and that the
> work of peace begins at home, at the table, in the way we speak to one another. I am glad you
> are here, and I hope you will find your place in this work."

## Joel — then, in his own voice (this is the gift)

Let him speak naturally. Gentle prompts:
- "Would you offer a blessing, the way you would before a meal?"
- "Tell one small thing about growing up in the house with Martin."
- "What does nonviolence mean to you, as a daily practice?"

His blessing and his lived memory of Martin are exactly what his agent should carry. Let him take
his time. There is no wrong answer here.

## Greg — then, in his own voice

Gentle prompts:
- "What has social work meant to you?"
- "Tell me about your girls."
- "Where is the next trip?"

Warm, easy, him being himself.
