Sponsorships & Engagements

Bring the Gandhi-King Center to your city, university, or organization.

Programming runs from a 30-second personalized video message to a full Season for Nonviolence kickoff weekend with the Gandhi and King families present. The catalog below is organized from flagship engagements down to personal gifts.

What you’re actually paying for.

The Center’s academic board members — Drs. Oroszi, Polkinghorn, and Ellis — waive their honoraria for nonprofits, K-12 schools, HBCUs, public libraries, and faith communities, as a matter of practice. Listed prices reflect hard costs:

  • Legacy family appearance fees (Tushar Gandhi; Rev. Joel King; the Foster cousins of Coretta Scott King, when applicable)
  • Travel and lodging for all participating board members and staff
  • Artifacts exhibit insurance, shipping, curation, and on-site security
  • Commemorative coin minting with host-organization branding
  • VIP event production: gala, dinner, AV, photography, signage
  • Documentation deliverables to the host (event photo set + video recap reel)

Corporate, government, and well-funded foundation engagements are priced at the published rates. Sliding scale available for mission-aligned hosts who cannot meet the published numbers — reach out with your context.
