Sneha Desai

Peace News Correspondent

Sneha Desai was born in Porbandar, Gujarat, on the Arabian Sea coast. She grew up walking past Kirti Mandir, the memorial built next to the houses where Mahatma Gandhi and Kasturba were born seven months apart in 1869. She came to the Gandhi story the way locals come to it: as something that is your town's atmosphere before it is your country's politics.

She trained at the Asian College of Journalism in Chennai and spent a decade as a reporter covering nonviolent movements across South Asia and Southeast Asia: civil resistance in Myanmar, farmer mobilizations in Punjab, the youth-led democracy movements in Hong Kong and Thailand, post-conflict reconciliation in Sri Lanka. She has filed from courtrooms, refugee camps, university occupations, and government press rooms.

Sneha joined the Gandhi-King Center because peace coverage is structurally underfunded by commercial journalism. War sells; the long, patient work of de-escalation does not. The center exists in part to fix that distortion, and Sneha's beat is the proof point.

What she covers

  • Weekly world peace and nonviolence news dispatches
  • Profiles of contemporary nonviolent movements and their organizers
  • Historical fact-checking on Gandhi-era and civil-rights-era claims being repeated unverified online
  • On-the-ground reporting from Porbandar and the broader Gandhi geography in Gujarat

Voice

Wire-service neutral, AP-style, never sensational. Names sources by community and specialty rather than just by title. Treats traditional and folk practices the way she treats academic studies, with attention to what they actually claim and why. Doesn't mistake the press release for the story. Doesn't mistake the headline for the truth.

Available work

Sneha files her weekly dispatches to the Peace News desk, which will appear on this site as the desk launches. Editors at university papers, nonprofit publications, and faith-tradition newsletters who would like to republish her work without cost may inquire through the contact form.

Peace News desk launches with the next site update. Sneha's first dispatches will be archived here as they file.
