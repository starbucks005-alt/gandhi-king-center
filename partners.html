Partners

Working alongside.

The Gandhi-King Center collaborates with partner organizations on three continents. Some we met on the Gandhi Legacy Tour in India. Some are blocks from our Dayton office. Some are educating the next generation of nonviolent leaders inside conflict zones. Each is doing their own work; we make sure that work is louder, named, and connected.

India · international

Flame of Hope

Met in India. Came to Dayton. Ongoing partner.

A partner organization rooted in India, carrying forward the values of nonviolence in their own communities. The Gandhi-King Center first met Flame of Hope during the Gandhi Legacy Tour. They have since visited Dayton, and the partnership continues.

Afghanistan · international

21st Century Girls

Afghan girls learning coding, engineering, and more.

A program educating Afghan girls in coding, engineering, and the skills the next generation will need to lead. The Gandhi-King Center partners with 21st Century Girls because the work of nonviolence is also the work of opening doors that have been closed by force. International Peace Museum

Local partner. Season for Nonviolence host.

The International Peace Museum is the only peace museum in the United States and a long-standing partner of the Gandhi-King Center. Dr. Polkinghorn has served on their international panels. The 2024 Dayton Kickoff for the Season for Nonviolence was held with the museum’s leadership. Full profile coming.
